# Play Store Release Notes

## Version 0.1.1 (build 12)

### English (en-US)

HudumaGuide TZ helps Tanzanians understand government and business processes more easily. This release includes searchable service guides, saved checklists, deadline reminders, a guided business setup roadmap, optional account sync, and low-data offline access for saved guides. HudumaGuide TZ is an independent guide and links users to official channels for final confirmation.

### Kiswahili (sw)

HudumaGuide TZ inasaidia Watanzania kuelewa huduma za serikali na hatua za biashara kwa urahisi. Toleo hili lina guide zinazotafutika, checklist zilizohifadhiwa, reminders za deadline, roadmap ya kuanzisha biashara, account sync ya hiari, na matumizi ya low-data kwa guide zilizohifadhiwa. HudumaGuide TZ ni mwongozo huru; hakiki taarifa za mwisho kupitia vyanzo rasmi.
